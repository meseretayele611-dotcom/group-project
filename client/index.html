About Us

Who We Are

We are an computer maintenance service team supporting students, academic staff, administrative offices and university facilities.

Our Mission

To provide fast, reliable and transparent computer maintenance services while reducing equipment downtime.

Vision & Goals

Our computer Maintenance Services

We provide maintenance and technical support for computers, printers, networks, cables and other computer equipment.

🖥️ Computer & Hardware

Desktop and laptop troubleshooting, hardware replacement, RAM, hard disk, motherboard and performance problems.

🖨️ Printer Maintenance

Printer troubleshooting, paper jams, printing problems, driver installation, cartridge and network printer support.

🌐 Network & Internet

LAN and Wi-Fi troubleshooting, internet connection problems, IP configuration and network connectivity.

📡 Router & Switch

Router configuration, switch setup, network device troubleshooting and connectivity management.

💿 Software & OS

Operating system installation, software configuration, updates, drivers and application troubleshooting.

📽️ Projector & Display

Projector setup, display problems, HDMI/VGA connections, classroom display and presentation equipment support.

🔧 General ICT Support

Technical inspection, equipment setup, troubleshooting and other ICT maintenance services requested by university users.
